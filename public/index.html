How to Play Thulla (Bhabhi)

🎯 Objective

The goal is to shed all of your cards. The first player to finish their cards wins 1st place! The last remaining player left with cards is declared the "Thulla" (Bhabhi) and loses the game.

♠️ The Opening Move

All cards are dealt equally. The player who holds the Ace of Spades (♠A) MUST lead with it on the very first trick.

⚖️ Following Suit

When a player leads a card of a suit, every other player in clockwise turn MUST follow that suit if they hold any card of it.

💥 What is a THULLA?

If you do NOT have any card of the led suit, you can throw ANY card from your hand! That card is called a THULLA.

  • The player who threw the highest card of the led suit in that trick must pick up all cards currently on the table into their hand!
  • The player who picked up the cards starts (leads) the next round!

✨ Normal Trick Win (No Thulla)

If all active players follow suit without any Thulla, the player with the highest card of the led suit wins the trick. Those cards are permanently discarded to the dead pile, and the winner leads the next round!
